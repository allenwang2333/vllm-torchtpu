## Constraint sheet — area (4), layout and precision
`streamindex_topk` scoring kernel, GLM-5.2 DSA lightning indexer. **I choose nothing below.** Sign convention per `report.md`: percentages are changes in time, negative faster; no `Δ time %` column appears here, so directions are in words.

### 1. What this area forbids or requires at step 1's shapes

- **Block-shape rule.** Last two dims divisible by (8, 128) or equal to the array extent [verified: j-ed517191-5f2d-4d00-8c0c, `native_tile_granularity`]. Every trailing pair in the block clears it: scores block `(num_sublanes_bkv=16, 128)`, `q` block `(32, 128)`, `bq_weights` `(bq_sz, 32)` via the extent escape. `bkv_x2_ref`'s trailing pair is `(kv_packing=4, lkv_dim=256)` — **4 sublanes for an 8-bit type whose reported tiling is 32.** The (8,128) rule is *necessary and not sufficient* and an int8 block needs ≥32 rows [verified: j-106c8a46-174c-4c03-a76a, `block_sublane_legality_by_dtype`]; float8/uint8 were **not swept**, and this is a VMEM scratch rather than a grid `BlockSpec`, so whether the rule binds here is open. Requires a probe before anyone re-lays that scratch.
- **Requires: `pl.multiple_of` if the scores buffer is narrowed to bf16.** `start_send_scores` (L137-151) DMAs to `scores_hbm_ref.at[pl.ds(token_start, …)]` with `token_start` traced out of SMEM. f32 accepts all traced offsets; **bf16 rejects all four tested offsets, including 0**, and `pl.multiple_of(offset, 64)` is the whole fix [verified: j-106c8a46-174c-4c03-a76a, `traced_dma_offset_by_dtype`]. Any bf16-scores option carries this line or it does not compile.
- **Forbids: an int8 scores buffer produced by arithmetic.** `Only vector<i16> and vector<i32> are supported, but got 'i8'` [verified: j-a5c66200-aab5-4319-be73, `sublane_tiling_by_dtype`]. Storage support ≠ arithmetic support. i16 and i32 do lower.
- **Forbids: reading the used 129 bytes out of the 256-byte record with a strided vector read.** `x[::2]` / `ref[::2]` **do not compile**, three distinct refusals, both shapes [verified: j-054e7ac4-4099-4176-ad2b]. The narrowing has to be a DMA or a format change, not a strided load.
- **Requires: block ≥ ~2 MiB / above the 32 KiB knee.** Per-step fixed cost dominates below ~32 KiB per block [verified: j-ed517191-5f2d-4d00-8c0c, `native_tile_granularity`; corroborated by `block_size_bandwidth_curve` at 4% of peak]. The kernel violates this on three transfers: the scores store is **4 descriptors of 8192 B** at `decode_prod` (pitch 40960 B, one per token row), the `q` DMA is **4096 B** per decode token, and the `indexer_weights` DMA is **128 B** per decode token. `Deciding` #9 applies verbatim: a logically large block laid out with a stride is issued as many small descriptors.
- **Masking is licensed, padding is not.** Mask 1.01x, pad 1.03x [verified: j-050c7480-40a6-4a50-9e1b, `mask_versus_pad`]. The kernel already masks the ragged bkv tail (L351-352). It nonetheless *pads the allocation* (`num_sublanes_total` from `max_model_len`, L603-605) — a different decision, not covered by that result.
- **Where the masks apply, exactly** — the tail case AREAS warns about. Columns **inside** the written region are masked by `valid_mask ∧ causal_mask` at L351. Columns **beyond** the last bkv block up to `num_sublanes_total·128` are never touched by the kernel and are correct only because of the `jnp.full(-inf)` init at L745 aliased in via `input_output_aliases`. A second, independent mask exists downstream: `_effective_row_lengths` (L482-506) hands `sparsecore_topk` a per-row length. At `decode_prod` `row_lengths = 8192` and the written region is exactly 4×2048 = 8192 — **zero slack**. Removing the `-inf` init as an optimization makes correctness depend entirely on that equality holding at every regime.
- **`-inf` is legal here.** The reference's rule to use `-0.7·finfo(f32).max` [source: `.../splash_attention/base.py:28-30@9da2369`] is scoped to a mask applied *before an exponential*; these scores feed a top-k, not a softmax. The constraint it does impose: any precision option must represent `-inf` or an order-preserving minimum (rules out unsigned/integer encodings without an explicit sentinel).

### 2. Tagged facts from the reference file that bear on this block

**fp8 on the MXU — what is known and what is not.**
- `float8_e4m3fn` and `float8_e5m2` are **reported by the chip as natively supported matmul dtypes**, in all 12 sensible combinations with f32 and bf16; `int8` appears in no pair despite measuring 746.8 Tops/s, so the list is a lower bound [verified: j-4fba8dc6-d72c-41f9-948b, `tpu_info_reported_constants`].
- **fp8 matmul measured at 1420 Tops/s, 1.86x bf16** [`05-compute-units.md`, `mxu_peak_by_dtype`, cited in 04].
- A **block** of `float8_e4m3fn` loads and stores [verified: j-ed517191-5f2d-4d00-8c0c, `bitcast_and_subbyte`] — and 04 states plainly that this "is a much narrower claim than it looks": storage and arithmetic are separate questions, and `bitcast_and_subbyte` only answers the first.
- `DotAlgorithmPreset` is unavailable in Pallas-Mosaic-TPU; `_F32_DOT_PRECISION_MAP` covers **f32 operands only** [source: tokamax@9da2369]. The einsum at L326-331 passes neither `precision` nor an algorithm, only `preferred_element_type=jnp.float32`, on two fp8 operands. **Nothing in the reference file establishes what Mosaic emits for that.** Also on the record: `gmm_v2`/`tgmm_v2` accept a `precision` argument and **silently discard it** [source: `tokamax/_src/ops/experimental/gmm_v2/@9da2369`] — a precision knob that lands is a thing to verify, not assume.
- **What would have to be probed:** a Pallas `jnp.einsum("bnd,bmd->bnm", fp8, fp8, preferred_element_type=f32)` at the block's own shapes, timed against the same einsum with both operands pre-cast to bf16 in VMEM, and read back through `/xprof-llo` for the MXU pass count and operand dtype in the Mosaic MLIR. The two-arm timing alone is ambiguous if fp8 promotes silently; the LLO matmul count disambiguates it.

**Sub-32-bit scores.**
- **Dtype conversion is bandwidth, not arithmetic**: `int32 → float32` at equal width costs 1.00x, which is the control that proves the conversion itself is free; bf16→bf16 is **0.74x for 0.50 the bytes** and int8→bf16 **0.70x for 0.38** [verified: j-ed517191-5f2d-4d00-8c0c, `dtype_conversion_cost`]. **Halving a dtype buys about a quarter, not a half** at these block sizes; the fixed per-block cost eats the rest and would shrink at larger blocks.
- The tokamax rule this measures: **keep HBM narrow and widen per-tile in VMEM** [source: tokamax TPU kernels@9da2369].
- bf16 does **not** need 16-row blocks despite a reported tiling of 16 [verified: j-106c8a46-174c-4c03-a76a], and f32/bf16 sublane curves are identical to 3% [verified: j-a5c66200-aab5-4319-be73, reproduced from j-050c7480-40a6-4a50-9e1b]. So a bf16 scores block at `(16,128)` hits no legality wall.
- `pltpu.bitcast` f32→i32 works [verified: j-ed517191-5f2d-4d00-8c0c, `bitcast_and_subbyte`]; **no measurement of `pltpu.bitcast` on sub-byte types** exists.

**Moves.**
- RHS transpose / `dot_general` dimension numbers: 0.96x, i.e. **no measurable difference**, and it is free to adopt [verified: j-050c7480-40a6-4a50-9e1b, `transpose_cost`; the pattern is `NN_DIM_NUMBERS`/`NT_DIM_NUMBERS`, source: `.../splash_attention_kernel.py:45-46,452-457@9da2369`]. **Scope: every arm hands the value to `dot_general`.**
- Off the MXU it is different: **`swapaxes(-1,-2)` costs 0.79-0.82 ps/elem, +430 ns on a 644 ns step** at 2048×256; leading-axis add/drop/permute is **free**; a lane-boundary slice at a multiple of 128 is **free**; roll by a whole tile is free on either axis, roll by one costs 212-420 ns on either; `jnp.repeat(…, axis=-1)` **does not compile** [verified: j-054e7ac4-4099-4176-ad2b shards 0-1]. **f32 only — the bf16 sheet is unusable, its `identity_2d` control measured 785.5 ns against f32's 644.1.**
- LHS transpose routes through the XLU and tokamax pays a 1.5x VMEM budget (`num_buffers + 1`) to cache it [source: `tokamax/_src/ops/experimental/gmm_v2/tgmm_v2.py:117-126@9da2369`] — **[unverified] as a timing claim; treat as expensive until measured.**
- Reduction axis: lane 311.0us vs sublane 313.5us, **1.01x, reduce over whichever axis the algorithm wants** [verified: j-ed517191-5f2d-4d00-8c0c, `lane_vs_sublane_reduction`].
- **Below 256 lanes the lane extent is free** — an 8-lane block costs what a 256-lane block costs, total time flat to 3% while data grows 32x [verified: j-ed517191-5f2d-4d00-8c0c]. This is the fact under the `indexer_weights` lane-occupancy row below: 32 lanes of 128 costs the same as 128.
- Masks must be built at rank with `broadcasted_iota`; **`(BT,) -> (BT, 1)` on a bool tensor does not lower** [source: `.../kda/pallas_mosaic_tpu_fwd_kernel.py:686-695@9da2369`]. The kernel dodges this: L344-349 broadcast **int** arrays with `[None,None,:]` and compare, producing bool already at rank 3.

**Where the reference file and `report.md` disagree — stated, not reconciled.** `report.md`'s roofline uses V7X.md **spec** rates of 1785.6 TFLOP/s fp8 and 892.8 bf16, a ratio of 2.00x. `04`/`05` carry **measured** rates of 1420 Tops/s fp8 at **1.86x** bf16 [verified: j-ed517191-5f2d-4d00-8c0c, `mxu_peak_by_dtype`]. Both the absolute rates and the ratio differ; measured fp8 is 79.5% of spec. Arithmetic consequence (`modelled`, from `report.md`'s implied 6.87e10 score FLOPs at `mixed_prod`): the fp8 MXU term is 38.5 µs at the spec rate and **48.4 µs at the measured rate**, and the bf16 term 77.0 µs vs 103.9 µs. That moves `mixed_prod`'s achieved-against-fp8-floor from 14% to 18%. It does not change which regime is MXU- vs HBM-bound, and it does not change the roofline gate.

**Precision**
- **P1** Incumbent: fp8 einsum operands, f32 accumulate, f32 scores, 256-B u8 cache record, i32 output.
- **P2** Split `cache_kv` into a `[pages, 256, 4, 128]` u8 value array and a separate scale array — record 128 B/position, KV read waste 0%, halves 134.2 MB at `decode_prod`. Changes the scatter at `mla_attention_op.py:103-104` and the cache spec at `:235-236`.
- **P3** Keep one array but hoist scales: 128 value bytes per position plus one 128-B scale row per 128 positions → 128.0078 B/position, 0.006% waste, one contiguous record layout, still one DMA.
- **P4** Spend the existing pad on accuracy instead of bytes — 4 sub-block scales (132 of 256 B used). An accuracy trade, not a byte saving; on the record so the "pad is pure loss" framing is not the only one considered.
- **P5** Scores in `bfloat16` — halves 276.8 MB×3 at `mixed_64k`; expect about a quarter of the time back, not a half [verified: j-ed517191, `dtype_conversion_cost`]. Requires `pl.multiple_of` on the traced store offset, requires `sparsecore_topk` to select on bf16 (or the upcast at L820-821 reintroduces a full-width f32 pass), and makes rows 4/9/12 of the ledger **bf16 moves, which the relayout table does not measure**.
- **P6** Scores in `float8_e4m3fn`/`e5m2` — quarter the bytes; 3 and 2 mantissa bits against a selection whose measured disagreement with float64 is already rank-boundary churn within 61 ranks of a cut at 2048 of 8192 [`report.md`]. Enumerated so it is rejected on the record for a reason rather than absent.
- **P7** Scores as `int16`/`int32` under an order-preserving monotone encoding of the f32 bit pattern. The kernel returns **only indices** — the score value is never consumed downstream — so order is the entire contract. `vector<i16>` and `vector<i32>` are the two Mosaic supports [verified: j-a5c66200]; `-inf` becomes `INT_MIN`. i16 halves the bytes with no mantissa loss inside a fixed exponent range.
- **P8** Cast both fp8 operands to bf16 in VMEM before the einsum — removes the fp8-lowering uncertainty at (measured) 1/1.86 of the MXU rate. Only interesting if the probe says fp8 does not lower natively today.
- **P9** bf16 for the ReLU / per-head scale / 32-head sum chain (currently f32 in VMEM) — halves the traffic of the block's largest unpriced term, whose element counts are 1.7e7 at `decode_prod` and 2.7e8 at `mixed_prod`.
- **P10** `indexer_weights` in bf16 — halves 8.2 kB. Dismissed on size, and it would quantize the folded q fp8 scale (`mla_attention_op.py:278`).
- **P11** Top-k output as u16 — fits below 65536 positions, does not at `mixed_64k`'s 67584. Bounded by `sparsecore_topk`'s contract.

**Layout**
- **L1** Incumbent: bkv on the einsum output's lane axis; scale `.T`; scores split lane→sublane at the store.
- **L2** Emit `bmn` (bkv on sublanes) via `dot_general` dimension numbers rather than the array shapes — kills ledger row 4's `.T` and possibly row 12's split; makes the head-sum a lane reduction (1.01x, no difference [verified: j-ed517191]); makes bkv the **LHS**, which is the unmeasured-XLU gap and tokamax's 1.5x VMEM budget [source: `tgmm_v2.py:117-126@9da2369`].
- **L3** Reshape `bkv` in VMEM to `(16, 128, head_dim)` and contract in 3-D so the scores land already `(…,16,128)` — removes ledger row 12 entirely by construction.
- **L4** Have the kernel write the 2-D `(ntok, num_sublanes_total·128)` view directly, moving the split from inside the kernel to nowhere — but the HBM store stays one 8 kB strided descriptor per token row.
- **L5** Transpose the scores buffer to positions-major, `(num_sublanes_total, 128, ntok)` — the kernel's store becomes one contiguous 32 kB descriptor per bkv block instead of 4×8 kB, landing on the right side of the knee. Requires `sparsecore_topk` to select along a non-minor axis.
- **L6** Derive `num_sublanes_total` from the batch's actual `max(kv_len)` rather than `max_model_len` (L603-605), bucketed to keep the jit-static output shape to a few values. Kills the 20.0% padding at `decode_prod` and, more importantly, the case `report.md` flags as an A/B confound: a 64k `max_model_len` widening the buffer for every short request.
- **L7** Initialize only the pad sublanes with `-inf` instead of the whole buffer — removes 80.0% of a 2.62 MB write at `decode_prod` and 97.0% of a 276.8 MB write at `mixed_64k`. Correctness rests on the L351 mask covering everything inside the written region and on `_effective_row_lengths` never exceeding it — which at `decode_prod` is an exact equality with zero slack.
- **L8** Store the 128 scale bytes of a 128-position group as one lane-contiguous row in the cache, so `load_bkv` reads the scale already on the lane axis and row 4's `.T` disappears without changing precision.
- **L9** Store `cache_kv` position-minor, `(pages, lkv_dim, positions)` — values arrive with head_dim on sublanes and positions on lanes, matching the contraction and putting the scale on a single sublane. Turns the writer at `mla_attention_op.py:104` into a lane scatter.
- **L10** Narrow the *read* without touching the writer: DMA `[..., :128]` and fetch the scale separately. The scale fetch is a 1-byte-per-256-byte gather — the pattern that **does not compile** as a strided read; as a DMA it is 1-byte descriptors. This is where the no-writer-change option dies, and it should die on the record.
- **L11** Two DMAs per record group: a 2-D descriptor of 128 B rows at a 256 B pitch for values, plus one for scales. Halves the payload for 2× the descriptors at 128 B each. Whether the DMA engine bills the pitch or the payload is **`unmodelled`**.
- **L12** Store `q` flat as `(ntok·32, 128)` in HBM, removing ledger row 6's in-VMEM merge at mixed.
- **L13** Store `indexer_weights` transposed as `(32, ntok)` — heads on sublanes — removing ledger row 9's lane→sublane move.
- **L14** Narrow the SMEM metadata to i16. Dismissed on budget: worst case 16.6 kB against ~1 MB.
- **L15** Address the 4× MXU derate at decode (M = bq_sz·32 = 32 rows into a 128-row tile, `report.md`) by restructuring the `seq_batch_size=4` batching so the four sequences share an M axis. Each sequence has a different RHS, so this is not a layout move inside this area — it is an algebra question, `seams.md § S1` / `06-algorithm.md`, and is listed here only so the seam is visible from this sheet.
- **L16** Fold `indexer_weights` into `q` before the kernel (`relu(a)·w = relu(a·w)` **only for `w ≥ 0`**, and `w` is a linear projection's output times a positive scale, so the sign is unconstrained). Removes an operand and ledger row 9. Also an algebra question, `seams.md § S1`.
