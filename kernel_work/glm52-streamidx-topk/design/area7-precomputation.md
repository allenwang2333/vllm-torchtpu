# Area (7) constraint sheet — `streamindex_topk` / `sparsecore_topk` on GLM-5.2

**Scope, per `AREAS.md`:** scalar and index metadata plus grid-invariant tables only. Recompute-vs-save for *tensors* is area (1); recomputing an activation is area (6). Tensor-shaped candidates I found and am **handing off, not deciding**: the `f32[T,n]` score matrix's HBM round trip → (1); `jnp.take_along_axis` of `[b, p·k_p]` out of `[b, n]` in `sparsecore_topk` stage 2 (128 MiB at B-P1024) → (1)/(5); `prepare_q_inputs`' head-dim pad → (4); the structural `n = max_model_len` behind Finding 2 → (2)/(6). I own only the *scalars that size them*. **Level 2 (model load) is N/A** — nothing in this block is weight-derived; on the record as a decision.

### The three findings that carry

**1. 42 of 63 pallas launches per model step run a zero-extent grid, and all 63 pay a dynamic-grid fixed cost.** All three launches take *traced* `start/end_seq_idx`, so the measured **26.2 µs fixed + 0.427 µs/step** fit applies to each. At `A-D64-8k` that is 78.6 µs against a 363.2 µs measurement — **22% of the layer**, of which 14% is the two launches that do nothing. Cheapest probe on the sheet; the quantity ("is this range empty") is layer-invariant, available at level 3, and the spend is a `lax.cond` skip. Merging the three into one is (2)'s.

**2. The `-inf` fill is not merely hoistable, it looks dead.** SC never reads past `i_hi = min(slice_len, eff_len)`, and the launches write every block `[0, num_bkv·bkv_sz)` with `num_bkv·bkv_sz ≥ kv_len ≥ eff_len` for every row with `eff_len > 0` — so no position SC can select is ever left at the initializer's value. `[modelled, by reading the write set against `load_keys`' bound; not probed, and register item 2 questions whether XLA materializes the fill at all]`. Even if it is materialized it is **≤2.3% of measured time at every shape** — the honest quantification is that it is an HBM **capacity** term (4 GiB live per invocation, register item 4), not a latency one. Removal is (1)'s call; my row is that it is a run-invariant constant recomputed 21× per step.

**3. Per-grid-step metadata is not the missing 685 ns/KV-block.** The masks cost 20–459 ns per inner iteration against 4.5 / 4.4 / 24.9 µs of measured per-iteration time — **≤1.9% at every shape.** The scalar-core page-index arithmetic is the one area-(7) term that could be a real share of it: ~6% of the step at both decode shapes `[assumed]`. This is the row where quantifying beat assuming, in both directions.

### SMEM budget at (B) — half spent before anything is added

`page_indices` i32[131072] = **524,288 B** + `seq_lens` 256 + `cu_q_lens` 260 + 28 B control = **524,832 B = 512.5 KiB** against the **1,046,016 B (1021.5 KiB)** actually addressable `[verified: j-ed517191, smem_scratch_bisect]` — **50.2% consumed, 509 KiB headroom.** Consequence: an equal-sized level-3 offset table must **replace** `page_indices`, not join it. At (A) the same total is 2.5 KiB and SMEM is not a constraint at all. `int16` narrowing is **unavailable at (B)**: page values index `total_num_pages ≥ 64 × 2048 = 131072`, past both `int16` and `uint16`. At B-D4 only 4 of 64 sequences are live, so **8 KiB is read and 504 KiB is transferred and never touched**, 63 times per step = 31.5 MiB ≈ 8.5 µs `[modelled]`.

### What area (7) forbids and requires here

- **Forbids** hoisting anything derived from `seq_lens`, `cu_q_lens`, `page_indices` or `distribution` above level 3. vLLM's batch shape and page table change every step; level 1 or 2 is a silent wrong answer on the shape nobody tested.
- **Forbids** the splash `functools.lru_cache` trace-time idiom for any of them — it is legal there because the mask depends on static shape, and every candidate here depends on batch content.
- **Forbids** adding an SMEM table at (B) without displacing one (512.5 + 512 > 1021.5 KiB), and forbids `int16` `page_indices` at (B) on value range.
- **Requires** `p`, `slice_len`, `num_waves` and `num_sublanes_total` stay at level 1 — they set static shapes and cannot legally move.
- **Requires** the static-max-plus-runtime-count pattern for anything new: allocate at the padded maximum, transfer the true count (`traced_dma_length` is `[verified]`).
- **Requires** an answer on whether the 21 layers CSE *at all* before any level-3 hoist is costed, because that factor is 21.

### `## Gaps` this block turns on — each with the decision it settles

1. **"The CSE result did not isolate what it was meant to."** *Settles:* whether every level-3 row above costs ×1 or ×21. The reference's probe was 4 `pallas_call`s in **one jit**; here each layer is a separately-named torch custom op (`pallas::streamidx_topk_{prefix}`) built by its own `VllmTPUSparseAttnIndexer`, wrapping its own `jax.jit`, so cross-layer CSE is likely **structurally unavailable** and every metadata build inside the op is duplicated 21× by construction. *Under "CSE holds":* do nothing, the hoists are already free. *Under "it does not":* build all shape metadata once in `AttentionMetadata` and pass the *identical tensor* to all 21 layers. **Probe:** the one the gap names — distinct weight operands, identical metadata — at 21 calls through the torch custom-op boundary, not 4 inside one jit.
2. **"Where the dynamic grid's 26.2 µs fixed cost comes from is unknown — allocation or invocation."** *Settles:* the opposite change at (B). *Allocation-scaled:* capping the 4 GiB score buffer is the lever and the 42 empty launches cost little. *Invocation-scaled:* the empty-launch skip is the lever and the allocation matters only for HBM capacity. This block has both a 4 GiB allocation and 63 launches at grid extents 0–16, so it is the discriminating case. **Probe:** one compiled kernel, grid 0 vs 1, at two output allocations.
3. **"`SmemWrapper` is quoted but never exercised."** *Settles:* whether the hand-strided index `page_indices_ref[(seq+b)·pages_per_seq + kv_p_start + i]` — the ~6%-of-step row — is as cheap as a flat 1-D read. *Under "as cheap":* leave it. *Under "not":* the flat byte-offset table is free in bytes and removes the stride multiply.
4. **"Nothing here measures metadata at a realistic vLLM decode shape."** This block *is* that shape: 512 KiB of scalar prefetch, 63 launches/step, a 21× multiplier. **Every figure on this sheet resting on the 6.9 ns/scalar slope or the 26.2 µs fixed cost is an extrapolation into a regime the reference has never measured.**

**Do not count:** the narrow-SMEM-dtype capacity gap — `int16` is ruled out at (B) by value range, so no decision turns on whether it saves bytes. And the "recompute cost is a lower bound, possibly 5×" gap — `k_span` is genuinely iota-derived here, the folding-favourable case applies, and the masks are costed at ≤1.9% regardless.

### Staleness hazard per hoist — the counterpart risk

- **`-inf` buffer reused across layers:** safe *within* a step (all 21 layers dirty the identical write set), **fatal across steps** — a step with the same `T` but different `seq_lens` leaves last step's real scores where `-inf` is required and the top-k returns 2048 valid-looking indices. Must be keyed on `(seq_lens, cu_q_lens, distribution, T)`, i.e. refreshed every step, never cached on `T`.
- **Page byte-offset table:** the page table changes every step as blocks are allocated and freed. Level 3 only. Cached on `pages_per_seq` it addresses freed pages — garbage scores, no crash.
- **`num_bkv` per-group table:** depends on `seq_lens` *content*, not shape. Stale → the loop under-runs and silently drops whole KV blocks. **Covered by an existing instrument:** Finding 5's churn metric puts a dropped block at ~25% of the true top-k against a 0.005 threshold. Say so when proposing it.
- **`eff_row_lengths` / `lengths_p` moved to per-step:** same class; safe only if they land where `AttentionMetadata` is already rebuilt each step.
- **Bucketing `n` below `max_model_len`** (not mine to choose, but mine to warn about): a bucket **below** the live max is a correctness bug — positions past `n_bucket` become unreachable and the top-k is wrong with no error. Needs `min` against the actual and a runtime assert, and it is the one candidate here that fails silently in the *shrinking* direction.
- **Empty-launch skip:** a `lax.cond` on a traced `distribution` cannot go stale. Lowest-hazard, highest-value item on the sheet.
- **Dropping the dead index [1] from `num_sublanes_total`:** no staleness, but re-enabling the prefill range later silently restores the coupling. Take the max over only the indices `_common_path` reads, and assert the dead slot is unused.

### Placements considered and not favoured — one line each, **no choice made**

- **Level 1 for `num_bkv` / page offsets / `eff_row_lengths`** — depends on batch content, not static shape; exactly the correctness bug the area warns about.
- **Level 2 for anything** — nothing here is weight-derived; genuinely N/A.
- **Level 4 (in-kernel SMEM scratch) for the page byte-offset table** — 131,072 entries at 6.9 ns/scalar ≈ 904 µs of serial scalar-core fill per invocation, far past the "few thousand entries" boundary; the reference sends this size to level 3 because XLA parallelizes the scan and the scalar core does not.
- **Level 4 for `num_bkv`** — only ~16 entries, where levels 3 and 4 measured 0.96×/1.07×; no speed reason either way, and level 3 composes with the length-sorting Finding 3 wants.
- **Tracing the SC stage-1 DMA extent to `i_hi` instead of the static `slice_len`** — legal (`traced_dma_length` is `[verified]`), but **checked and rejected**: the `@pl.when(i_hi > 0)` guard plus per-partition `lengths_p` already bound the transfer to the live prefix, so it recovers ~1.5% of traffic and nothing else.
- **Raising `MAX_SLICE_WORDS` to halve `p`** — ruled out by the 480 KiB scratch ceiling, above.
- **`num_scalar_prefetch` driving a `BlockSpec` `index_map`** — the reference's highest-value spend, but this kernel uses manual `make_async_copy` with `pl.ds(traced_offset, …)`; reaching it is a pipeline restructuring owned by (2)/(3), not a placement I can take.
- **A `pltpu.BufferedRef` subclass reading offsets from a metadata ref** — same reason; it would replace the hand-rolled double buffer, which is (3)'s call.
- **VMEM instead of SMEM for the offset table** — competes with the block sizes (2) already spent, and the reference measured the VMEM middle rung recovering only 13% of the available gap.
- **Hoisting `_effective_row_lengths` for its own cost** — measured out at <0.001% of the step at all three shapes; worth moving only as part of the CSE-barrier fix, never on its own.
- **Sizing `len_vmem` by `num_waves`** — correct and cheap, but 0.012% of time; take it for the 62 KiB of SC VMEM, not for latency, and note it unlocks nothing downstream.
- **Removing the dead `distribution[1]` slot from `num_sublanes_total`** — zero bytes at today's `(2,2,2)`; a prerequisite for per-case `bkv_p` tuning, not a saving.
